Dealex Own Terminal (DOT)
Reefer & Cold Chain HUB

Dealex Own Terminal (DOT) is a purpose-built Reefer & Cold Chain logistics hub designed to handle, store, monitor, and service temperature-controlled containers with the highest standards of efficiency, safety, and reliability. Developed to meet the growing needs of perishable cargo movement, DOT ensures end-to-end cold chain integrity from container arrival to final dispatch.

Reefer Container Stacking & Storage

Secure and organized stacking solutions supported by efficient yard planning for both short-term and long-term storage requirements.

Reefer Plug-in & Power Supply Facilities

High-capacity plug-in points providing uninterrupted power supply to maintain precise temperature control at all times.

24/7 Reefer Monitoring

Round-the-clock monitoring of temperature, humidity, and container performance by trained and certified reefer technicians.

Pre-Trip Inspection (PTI)

Comprehensive PTI services to ensure reefer containers are fully compliant and ready for loading and onward transit.

Technical Support & Repairs

On-site reefer mechanics equipped to handle troubleshooting, preventive maintenance, and both minor and major repairs.

Cold Chain Integrity Management

Strict operational protocols and monitoring systems designed to protect cargo quality throughout the storage and handling process.
