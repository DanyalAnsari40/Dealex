Our Heritage & Mission

Engineered for Precision Freight & Cold Chain Logistics

Dealex Transport & Logistics Services (DTLS) was built to revolutionize freight forwarding and cold chain management in Pakistan. We bridge critical supply chain gaps by integrating high-capacity reefer storage, multi-modal transport, and real-time tracking.

Through continuous innovation, custom terminal infrastructure (DOT), and strict thermal compliance, we empower food exporters, pharmaceutical companies, and industrial giants to transport goods safely across international trade lanes.
