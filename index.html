Ask Us a Question →
What container sizes do you support? ↘

We provide transportation services for standard reefer/dry container sizes, including 20ft and 40ft containers, subject to availability.

Can you maintain the required temperature during transportation? ↘

Yes. Our reefer transportation operations are designed to support the required temperature conditions and minimize the risk of temperature deviation during transit.

Do you provide door-to-door and port-to-terminal reefer transportation? ↘

Yes. We provide reefer container transportation from designated pickup locations, including ports, terminals, warehouses, and cold storage facilities, to the required delivery point, depending on the shipment requirements.

How do I book a reefer/ dry transportation service? ↘

You can contact our team with your container details, pickup location, delivery location, required temperature, date, and other shipment requirements. Our team will coordinate the transportation accordingly.

What information is required to get a quotation? ↘

Typically, we require the container size, pickup and delivery locations, cargo type, required temperature, preferred date/time, and any special handling requirements.

Do you provide reefer transportation for both import and export containers? ↘

Yes, our services can support both import and export reefer movements, subject to the specific shipment requirements.

Do you provide transportation for empty reefer containers? ↘

Yes, empty reefer container movements can also be arranged based on operational requirements.

How do you handle temperature-sensitive cargo? ↘

Our operations team coordinates the transportation according to the cargo's temperature requirements and follows appropriate procedures for reefer container handling.

Do you provide warehouse services for reefer and dry cargo? ↘

Yes. If you require warehouse services for your reefer or dry cargo, you can contact our team with your cargo details and storage requirements. Our team will assist you with suitable warehouse solutions based on your shipment needs.

What is reefer container transportation and what types of cargo can you transport? ↘

Reefer container transportation is the movement of temperature-sensitive cargo in refrigerated containers while maintaining the required temperature throughout the journey. We handle a wide range of perishable and temperature-sensitive cargo, including fruits, vegetables, dairy products, frozen food, meat, seafood, pharmaceuticals, and other suitable goods.
